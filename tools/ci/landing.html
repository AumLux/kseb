What's inside

One app for the whole contractor team, from line staff to the director.

GPS attendance

Check in and out within the section geofence. Managers see where and when on a map, with leave and muster roll built in.

Worksheets

Plan jobs, assign crews, run the permit-to-work checklist and attach geotagged photos, with approvals end to end.
